PAW EVENTS (a key with value 1 at the moment it happens, once): right_let_go / left_let_go = that paw lets go: the thing flies off with the paw's own motion, falls under gravity, bounces or rests where it lands, unless a paw catches it. right_toss_up / left_toss_up = <h> lets go AND throws it straight up h H above the paw (0.2 small toss .. 1.5 very high); never together with let_go. right_catch / left_catch = that paw is ready to catch: a flying thing that comes near it is caught (set it at the moment the thing arrives, with the paw where the thing comes down, open about 0.7). right_grab / left_grab = take the free thing nearest that paw (a thing it is touching or just caught on its body).
Flight time: a thing tossed h up comes back to the same height after 0.82·sqrt(h) s (h 0.3: 0.45 s, 0.5: 0.58 s, 0.8: 0.73 s, 1.2: 0.9 s); caught lower, a little later. Put the catch pose EXACTLY that long after the toss, never sooner. It comes down where it went up, so the catching paw must be there by then.
