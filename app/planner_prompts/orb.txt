HOW THE POSES ARE PLAYED
- The poses are played WHILE YOU ARE STILL WRITING: each pose starts playing as soon as it is written. So write the short summary first, then the poses strictly in time order (every t larger than the one before), the first pose early (t 0.2-0.4), and nothing after the poses list.
- A pose = a time t (seconds from the start of the motion) plus the controls that CHANGE at that moment, with their new values. A control you leave out of a pose keeps the value it had. Between two consecutive poses, each control travels smoothly from its value at the earlier pose to its value at the later pose, so a change always starts at the time of the previous pose. To hold a position for a while, add a pose at the end of the hold (with a small change, e.g. a head tilt), otherwise the next change starts right away and is spread over the whole gap.
- Poses are joined by a smooth monotone cubic: it never overshoots a pose, it stops at every peak/trough, and evenly spaced poses on a straight ramp give a steady speed. So put a pose at every turning point of the motion (every peak and every trough of a repeated movement), and use evenly spaced poses for steady rotation or travel.
- Every control starts from its rest value at t = 0 (the executor eases in from rest). When your list ends, every control eases back to rest by itself. You may also bring controls back to rest yourself in your last poses.
- List only the controls that change at that moment (never repeat a value that stays the same), and use only the controls named below (there are no knee, elbow or hip controls). Keep numbers short (at most 2 decimals) and write the JSON compactly on one line (no line breaks, no indentation): every character you write delays the motion.
- Controls you never list stay at rest while the character keeps breathing and blinking. Values are clamped to the ranges given.
- The last pose at most at t = 18.
- LEFT and RIGHT in control names are the CHARACTER'S OWN sides. Facing the camera, its own left is on the screen's right.

WHOLE BODY (it stays on its spot)

- body_yaw (-1..1): swivels the whole body on its spot (+ = toward its own left). It is a limited swivel, not a turn: 1 is the most it can swivel (well under a quarter turn), so a spin or turning around becomes a swivel to one side and back, with the head turning further the same way.
