Skin detail maps for the ICT-FaceKit head ( junor), generated by scratch/head/skin/{fit,bake,process}.py

SOURCE (only scan data used):
  "Infinite, 3D Head Scan" by Lee Perry-Smith (Infinite-Realities, ir-ltd.net), based on a work at www.triplegangers.com
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0) - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Downloaded from the McGuire Computer Graphics Archive repackage (OBJ + lambertian.jpg 4096 colour + bump.png 6000 16-bit displacement):
     https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/research/model/lpshead/lpshead.zip   (original: http://www.ir-ltd.net/infinite-3d-head-scan-released)
  Same scan (lower-res maps) also ships in three.js: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith
  Required attribution text:
     "Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith (Infinite-Realities, www.ir-ltd.net), based on a work at www.triplegangers.com,
      licensed under CC BY 3.0. Modified: registered to the ICT-FaceKit topology, band-passed, beard/brow areas replaced with
      synthesized pore detail, re-baked into ICT UV space."
  What we changed: non-rigid registration onto ICT topology; maps re-baked into ICT UVs; low frequencies removed
  (only pore/fine-wrinkle detail kept); stubble/brow/stretched areas replaced by randomly tiled crops of the scan's own
  clean cheek detail + procedural pores; chroma compressed.
Tools: Google MediaPipe face_landmarker.task (Apache-2.0) for landmarks only (not shipped).
Target topology/UVs: ICT-FaceKit (MIT).
Rejected: Digital Emily 2 (Wikihuman/USC ICT) - non-commercial licence; FFHQ-UV - CC BY-NC-SA; MPFB2 textures dir had only masks.
